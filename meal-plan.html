Pizza Prep
Make Dough in the Afternoon (while kids nap / play)
Combine 2¼ tsp yeast + 1 cup warm water + 1 tsp sugar — let sit 5 min until foamy Mix in 3 cups flour + 1 tsp salt + 2 tbsp olive oil — knead 8 min, or use stand mixer Cover with a towel, let rise 1–2 hours at room temperature — then it's ready
🍕 Hands-off rise time = free afternoon
Dinner
🍕 Homemade Pizza Night — Everyone Builds Their Own
Divide risen dough into portions — 1 big for dad, 1 medium for mom, 3 small for kids Each person spreads their own sauce and picks toppings from the spread Dad: classic pepperoni + mozzarella, or whatever you're craving Mom: olive oil base + shrimp + roasted peppers + fresh mozzarella Kids: red sauce + cheese — they'll add whatever they feel brave about Bake at 475–500°F for 8–10 min on a preheated sheet pan or pizza stone
✅ All eat — everyone loves this 👦 Kids build their own = no complaints 🐟 Mom: shrimp + veggie pizza
💡 This is the weekly tradition — make it fun. Let the 7-year-old stretch their own dough.

⚡ Quick Tips for the Week

Sunday Prep (30 min)

Hard-boil 6 eggs, marinate Monday's chicken thighs, roast a double batch of veggies. Cuts active cooking time on your hardest weeknights down to nearly zero prep.

Dad's OMAD Strategy

Every dinner is protein-forward and sized up for you. Add an extra serving of whatever protein is on the menu and pile on the roasted vegetables — you're eating one great meal a day, make it count.

Kid Picky Eater Hack

Autonomy kills complaints. Taco night (Wed), pizza build (Sat), and tenders + dipping (Fri) all let them choose — and they almost always eat more when they built it themselves.

Mom's Pescatarian Nights

On chicken/beef nights (Mon, Wed, Fri), a 6–8 oz piece of fish goes in the same oven, same temperature. 5 extra minutes, zero extra dishes, no separate "mom meal" drama.

Saturday Pizza Timing

Make dough mid-afternoon during downtime. The 1–2 hour rise is hands-off. By dinner time it's ready, and letting the 7-year-old stretch their own dough round is genuinely fun — they'll ask for this every week.

Snack Prep Shortcut

Monday morning: wash all fruit, fill 5 small containers with carrots + hummus, set out cheese sticks. Done for the week. The 3-year-old can grab independently — that's a few minutes back in your afternoon.

Freshness Order

Use asparagus (Tue) and fresh greens early. Bell peppers, broccoli, zucchini hold all week. This means zero sad produce by Friday — and the Sunday roast cleans up whatever remains.

Kroger App

Check digital coupons before shopping. Salmon, chicken breast, and shrimp rotate through deals regularly. That alone can save $15–25/week on your biggest cost category.
